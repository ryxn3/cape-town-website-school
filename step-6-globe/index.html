Cape Town

(the Mother City)

I went to Cape Town with my family and it is honestly one of the most beautiful places I have ever been. There are mountains, beaches, penguins and really good food.

    Food you have to try

    • Gatsby a massive sandwich with chips inside. One is enough for like 3 people.
    • Bobotie spiced mince with an egg topping, usually with yellow rice.
    • Cape Malay curry sweet and mild curry, best in the Bo-Kaap.
    • Koeksisters fried dough in syrup. Very very sweet.
    • Fish and chips get it fresh in Kalk Bay or Hout Bay.

    Good to know

    Weather

    Summer is December to February. The wind (the "Cape Doctor") can be really strong so bring a jacket.

    Getting around

    Use the MyCiTi bus or Uber. Renting a car is best for Cape Point.

    Money

    The money is the Rand (R). Most places take card.

    Be careful

    Don't walk alone at night and keep your phone in your pocket.
