    Good to know

    Weather

    Summer is December to February. The wind (the "Cape Doctor") can be really strong so bring a jacket.

    Getting around

    Use the MyCiTi bus or Uber. Renting a car is best for Cape Point.

    Money

    The money is the Rand (R). Most places take card.

    Be careful

    Don't walk alone at night and keep your phone in your pocket.
