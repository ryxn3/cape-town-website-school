Welcome to Cape Town

Mountains, beaches, penguins and really good food. Here is my little guide to the Mother City.

See the places

Places to visit

These are the places you should not miss.

Table Mountain

The flat mountain you see from everywhere in the city. You can hike up or take the cable car to the top. The view is amazing.

Nature

Boulders Beach

A beach in Simon's Town full of African penguins. You can walk on the boardwalk and see them really close.

Animals

Bo-Kaap

A neighbourhood with super colourful houses and cobbled streets. It is the home of Cape Malay culture and food.

Culture

Cape Point

The tip of the Cape Peninsula with cliffs, an old lighthouse and baboons. Drive there on Chapman's Peak Drive for the views.

Nature

V&A Waterfront

The harbour with shops, restaurants, street music and the aquarium. Good place to go at sunset.

Shopping

Robben Island

The island where Nelson Mandela was in prison for 18 years. The ferry leaves from the Waterfront and the tour guides were prisoners there.

History

Kirstenbosch

A huge garden on the side of Table Mountain. Walk on the tree canopy walkway they call the Boomslang.

Nature

Camps Bay

White sand, palm trees and the Twelve Apostles mountains behind. The water is freezing though!

Beach

Food to try

  • Gatsby - a massive sandwich with chips inside. One is enough for like 3 people.
  • Bobotie - spiced mince with an egg topping, usually with yellow rice.
  • Cape Malay curry - sweet and mild curry, best in Bo-Kaap.
  • Koeksisters - fried dough in syrup. Very sweet.
  • Fish and chips - get it fresh in Kalk Bay or Hout Bay.

Good to know

☀ Weather

Summer is December to February. The wind (the "Cape Doctor") can be really strong so bring a jacket.

🚌 Getting around

Use the MyCiTi bus or Uber. Renting a car is best for Cape Point.

💰 Money

The money is the Rand (R). Most places take card.

⚠ Be careful

Don't walk alone at night and keep your phone in your pocket.
