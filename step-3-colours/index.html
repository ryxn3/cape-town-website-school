Welcome to Cape Town

Mountains, beaches, penguins and really good food. Here is my little guide to the Mother City.

See the places

Places to visit

Table Mountain

The flat mountain you see from everywhere in the city. You can hike up or take the cable car to the top.

Boulders Beach

A beach in Simon's Town full of African penguins.

Bo-Kaap

A neighbourhood with super colourful houses and cobbled streets.

Cape Point

The tip of the Cape Peninsula with cliffs, an old lighthouse and baboons.

V&A Waterfront

The harbour with shops, restaurants and the aquarium.

Food to try

  • Gatsby
  • Bobotie
  • Cape Malay curry
  • Koeksisters

Good to know

Summer is December to February. The wind can be really strong.
